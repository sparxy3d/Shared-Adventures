# Vendor Terms

**Version 1.0 · Effective 28 October 2026**

## 1. About these terms

1.1 These terms are an agreement between you, the business or person offering activities on Free Spirit ("you", "vendor"), and Free Spirit Club (Pvt) Ltd ("Free Spirit", "we", "us").

1.2 Free Spirit runs freespirit.club, a marketplace where people find and book activities to do together. You provide the activities. We help customers find you, take their payment, and pay you.

1.3 You accept these terms when you tick the box on the vendor application. We record the version you accepted and the date and time.

1.4 Some words we use:

- **Customer:** a person who books one of your activities through Free Spirit.
- **Listing:** an activity you offer on Free Spirit, with its description, photos, prices and schedule.
- **Session:** a specific date and time when a listing runs.
- **Booking:** a customer's reservation for a session, for a stated number of people.
- **Booking value:** the total the customer pays for a booking.
- **Dashboard:** your vendor account on Free Spirit.

1.5 If you sign up on behalf of a business, you confirm you are authorised to accept these terms for it.

## 2. Joining Free Spirit

2.1 **Applying.** You apply through the form on freespirit.club. We review every application and may approve or decline it at our discretion. Approval is not automatic.

2.2 **Verification.** To protect customers, we ask for:

- A copy of a valid national identity card or passport of the person responsible for the activity (required).
- A police clearance report (optional, but it may help your application, especially for activities with children or overnight stays).
- A Business Registration certificate, if your business is registered (optional).

See clause 8 for how we store and delete these documents.

2.3 **Who can join.** You must be at least 18, legally able to run your activity in Sri Lanka, and hold any licences, permits or certifications your activity needs. If any of these change, tell us straight away.

2.4 **Your account.** Keep your login details private. You are responsible for what happens in your dashboard. Tell us at hello@freespirit.club if you think someone else has accessed it.

2.5 **True information.** Everything you give us must be accurate and kept up to date. We may suspend your account if we find information is false or misleading.

## 3. Your listings

3.1 **As many as you like.** You can list as many activities as you run. Each listing has its own prices, schedule and photos.

3.2 **Accurate and current.** Your listings must describe the activity honestly: what customers do, how long it takes, where it happens, what is included, what to bring, and any age, fitness or safety requirements. Keep your schedule and available spots up to date so customers only book sessions you can run.

3.3 **Your prices.** You set your own prices per person. The price you list is the price the customer pays, and you may not charge customers anything extra for what the listing includes. You may set one price for Sri Lankans and residents of Sri Lanka (in rupees) and a different price for foreign visitors (in US dollars). Foreign visitors are charged the rupee equivalent of your US dollar price, at the exchange rate Free Spirit sets on the day of booking. You are paid in rupees.

3.4 **Bookings stay on Free Spirit.** Customers who find you through Free Spirit book and pay through Free Spirit. You must not:

- put phone numbers, email addresses, website links or social media handles in your listings, photos or messages before a booking is confirmed;
- ask or encourage customers who found you on Free Spirit to book or pay you directly instead, for that booking or for repeat visits arranged through us.

Once a booking is confirmed, we share your contact details with that customer so you can coordinate the session.

3.5 **Photos and content.** You confirm you own, or have permission to use, every photo, video and text you upload, and that people shown in your photos have agreed to appear. You give Free Spirit a free, non-exclusive licence to use that content to show and promote your listings on our website, social media and marketing, for as long as your listing is live and for a reasonable period afterwards for content already published.

3.6 **Our review.** We may edit listings for clarity or formatting, and may hide or remove a listing that breaks these terms, is misleading, or receives serious complaints. We will tell you why.

3.7 **Your Google rating.** If you give us a link to your Google Maps or Google Business page, we may show your Google rating and reviews on your listings, labelled as coming from Google.

## 4. Bookings and payments

4.1 **How a booking works.**

1. A customer chooses a session and the number of people, and sends a booking request.
2. Their card is authorised for the full booking value. The money is held on their card, not yet taken.
3. You accept or decline the request in your dashboard.
4. When you accept, the payment is taken automatically and the booking is confirmed. The customer and you are both notified.
5. If you decline, or do not respond in time, the hold is released and the customer is not charged.

4.2 **Responding in time.** You must accept or decline within 30 minutes for sessions starting within the next 3 hours, and within 12 hours for later sessions. Requests you do not answer in time are declined automatically. Regularly missing or declining requests for sessions you have listed as available may affect how your listings are shown.

4.3 **Only accept what you can run.** By accepting, you commit to running the session for that customer as listed.

4.4 **Payments go through us.** Customers pay Free Spirit, not you. Free Spirit Club (Pvt) Ltd is the merchant of record. Card payments are processed by our payment provider, PayHere. We never see or store full card numbers.

4.5 **Taking bookings switch.** You can switch "Taking bookings" off in your dashboard at any time. While it is off, your listings are hidden from search and no new requests can be made. Bookings you have already accepted still go ahead.

4.6 **Sri Lankan and resident rate.** Customers choose the rate for each person in their booking. Anyone booked at the Sri Lankan rate must show a national identity card, Sri Lankan passport or resident visa when they arrive, and you are responsible for checking it. If a guest cannot show proof, you may refuse that guest, and that guest's share of the booking is not refunded.

## 5. Our fee and your payouts

5.1 **Our fee.** For each completed booking, we keep a fee (commission) on the booking value:

- **Introductory rate: 10%** for your first 3 months after your first listing goes live.
- **Standard rate: 15%** after that.

We may agree different rates with you when we approve your account. The rates that apply to you are always shown in your dashboard, with the date your introductory period ends. The introductory rate is a limited offer, not a permanent rate.

5.2 **The rate is fixed per booking.** Each booking records the fee rate in force when it was made. A later change to rates never changes bookings already made.

5.3 **Payment processing fees.** Free Spirit pays the card processing fee out of its own fee, so you receive the booking value minus our fee only.

5.4 **Changes to rates.** We may change fee rates by giving you at least 30 days' notice by email and in your dashboard. The new rate applies only to bookings made after the change takes effect. If you do not agree, you may close your account before then (clause 9).

5.5 **When you get paid.** We pay you weekly for sessions that took place in the previous week (Monday to Sunday), by bank transfer to the account you give us in your dashboard. The account must be in your name or your registered business's name.

5.6 **Statements.** Your dashboard shows every booking, its booking value, our fee, and the amount owed or paid to you, with the bank reference for each payout.

5.7 **Holding back payment.** We may delay or hold back payment for a booking if the customer has raised a complaint or a card dispute, if the session did not take place as listed, or if we reasonably suspect fraud. We will tell you why and resolve it as quickly as we can. Amounts we refund to customers under clause 6 are deducted from what we owe you.

5.8 **Your taxes.** You are responsible for your own income tax, VAT and any other taxes on what you earn.

## 6. Cancellations, no-shows and refunds

6.1 **Customer cancels.**

- More than 24 hours before the session: the customer gets a full refund. You receive nothing and we keep no fee.
- Within 24 hours of the session: no refund, unless you agree to one. You are paid as normal, minus our fee.

6.2 **Customer doesn't turn up.** A no-show is treated as a late cancellation. You are paid as normal, minus our fee. Mark the booking as a no-show in your dashboard within 24 hours of the session.

6.3 **You cancel.** If you cancel an accepted booking, for any reason, the customer gets a full refund and you receive nothing for that booking. Cancel only when you must, and as early as possible, through your dashboard so the customer is told straight away. Repeated cancellations may lead to your listings being paused or removed (clause 9).

6.4 **Weather and safety.** If you cancel because it is unsafe to run the activity (for example, weather or river conditions), the customer gets a full refund or may move to another session if you offer one. These cancellations do not count against you under 6.3, provided you tell us the reason.

6.5 **Activity not as described.** If a customer complains that a session did not take place, or was materially different from your listing, we will ask for your side before deciding. If we find the complaint justified, we may refund the customer in full or in part, and deduct that amount from what we owe you.

# Vendor Terms

**Version 1.0 · Effective 28 October 2026**

## 1. About these terms

1.1 These terms are an agreement between you, the business or person offering activities on Free Spirit ("you", "vendor"), and Free Spirit Club (Pvt) Ltd ("Free Spirit", "we", "us").

1.2 Free Spirit runs freespirit.club, a marketplace where people find and book activities to do together. You provide the activities. We help customers find you, take their payment, and pay you.

1.3 You accept these terms when you tick the box on the vendor application. We record the version you accepted and the date and time.

1.4 Some words we use:

- **Customer:** a person who books one of your activities through Free Spirit.
- **Listing:** an activity you offer on Free Spirit, with its description, photos, prices and schedule.
- **Session:** a specific date and time when a listing runs.
- **Booking:** a customer's reservation for a session, for a stated number of people.
- **Booking value:** the total the customer pays for a booking.
- **Dashboard:** your vendor account on Free Spirit.

1.5 If you sign up on behalf of a business, you confirm you are authorised to accept these terms for it.

## 2. Joining Free Spirit

2.1 **Applying.** You apply through the form on freespirit.club. We review every application and may approve or decline it at our discretion. Approval is not automatic.

2.2 **Verification.** To protect customers, we ask for:

- A copy of a valid national identity card or passport of the person responsible for the activity (required).
- A police clearance report (optional, but it may help your application, especially for activities with children or overnight stays).
- A Business Registration certificate, if your business is registered (optional).

See clause 8 for how we store and delete these documents.

2.3 **Who can join.** You must be at least 18, legally able to run your activity in Sri Lanka, and hold any licences, permits or certifications your activity needs. If any of these change, tell us straight away.

2.4 **Your account.** Keep your login details private. You are responsible for what happens in your dashboard. Tell us at hello@freespirit.club if you think someone else has accessed it.

2.5 **True information.** Everything you give us must be accurate and kept up to date. We may suspend your account if we find information is false or misleading.

## 3. Your listings

3.1 **As many as you like.** You can list as many activities as you run. Each listing has its own prices, schedule and photos.

3.2 **Accurate and current.** Your listings must describe the activity honestly: what customers do, how long it takes, where it happens, what is included, what to bring, and any age, fitness or safety requirements. Keep your schedule and available spots up to date so customers only book sessions you can run.

3.3 **Your prices.** You set your own prices per person. The price you list is the price the customer pays, and you may not charge customers anything extra for what the listing includes. You may set one price for Sri Lankans and residents of Sri Lanka (in rupees) and a different price for foreign visitors (in US dollars). Foreign visitors are charged the rupee equivalent of your US dollar price, at the exchange rate Free Spirit sets on the day of booking. You are paid in rupees.

3.4 **Bookings stay on Free Spirit.** Customers who find you through Free Spirit book and pay through Free Spirit. You must not:

- put phone numbers, email addresses, website links or social media handles in your listings, photos or messages before a booking is confirmed;
- ask or encourage customers who found you on Free Spirit to book or pay you directly instead, for that booking or for repeat visits arranged through us.

Once a booking is confirmed, we share your contact details with that customer so you can coordinate the session.

3.5 **Photos and content.** You confirm you own, or have permission to use, every photo, video and text you upload, and that people shown in your photos have agreed to appear. You give Free Spirit a free, non-exclusive licence to use that content to show and promote your listings on our website, social media and marketing, for as long as your listing is live and for a reasonable period afterwards for content already published.

3.6 **Our review.** We may edit listings for clarity or formatting, and may hide or remove a listing that breaks these terms, is misleading, or receives serious complaints. We will tell you why.

3.7 **Your Google rating.** If you give us a link to your Google Maps or Google Business page, we may show your Google rating and reviews on your listings, labelled as coming from Google.
